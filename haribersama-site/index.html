A growth plan for HariBersama.

Content planning, B2B outreach, and market analysis for a digital wedding invitation platform.

Problem

The platform needed a clearer target market, consistent content direction, and business partners to reach more potential customers.

What I did

  • Content planning: Planned topics and content direction for HariBersama's social media.
  • B2B outreach: Reached out to potential business partners to explore collaboration.
  • Market research: Researched customer needs, competitors, and partnership opportunities.
  • Market analysis: Used BMC to map customer segments, value propositions, channels, and key partners.

Tools

Business Model Canvas (BMC) · Instagram · Market and competitor research

Result/Insight

Developed content plans, initiated partnership outreach, and translated market research into BMC-based business direction.

Insight: content and partnerships should reinforce the same customer segment and value proposition.

Website and Instagram snapshots show the product and content channel behind this work.
