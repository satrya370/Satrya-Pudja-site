PT JTB Indonesia · Digital Marketing · 2024

Travel content built for SEO.

My blog project for Kura-Kura Bus: help travelers discover Bali tours through useful search-led content.

Problem

Improve Kura2Bus's SEO visibility and reach travelers researching Bali, before they are ready to choose a tour.

What I did

Published SEO-oriented travel articles on the Kura2Bus blog, connected them to relevant tour pages, and supported off-page promotion through backlink seeding and SEOClerks.

Copywriting: AIDA (attention → interest → desire → action), problem–solution openings, benefit-led headlines, and clear CTAs to explore a tour.

Tools

WordPress / Kura-Kura Blog · SEOClerks · Search-intent content planning · Backlink seeding

Result/Insight

Published Bali travel articles under my Satrya byline, with links and CTAs to tour products. The objective was stronger organic discovery; the public evidence is the published work.

Insight: answer the traveler's question first, then make the next step relevant and easy.
