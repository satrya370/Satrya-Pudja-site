The problem: creative variety without repetitive production

Producing many ad files is not the same as producing different ideas. The brief calls for at least 15 concepts with their own composition, typography, layout, and message. Changing only a headline or background color does not count as a new concept.

The planned approach separates creative thinking from image production: AI develops angles and Dutch copy, while reusable HTML/CSS templates produce the images. This avoids an AI call for every image and every size while preserving consistent LayerFit branding.

From brief to export

01 · Research & input

Start with the product and market

Collect the product photo, benefits, audience, and campaign brief. Research Dutch-market references in Meta Ad Library and document links with a short creative rationale. Ad longevity is a research signal, not proof of performance.

02 · AI strategy & copy

Generate angles, not individual images

Use as few AI calls as practical to develop creative angles and Dutch-language text. Track token usage and estimated API cost per batch and per creative.

03 · Template rendering

Make the concepts visually distinct

Render product imagery and text through HTML/CSS layouts, without an AI image-generation call per output. Adapt each concept to square, portrait, and vertical placements.

04 · Dashboard review

Keep a human in control

Provide a creative gallery with favorites, editable text, product-photo replacement, individual regeneration, and previews for Meta placements.

05 · Batch export

Prepare files for campaign use

Export PNG or JPG images at 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350, and 1080 × 1920, with a bulk ZIP download. Check text overflow and placement-safe areas before export.

Delivery targets, not claimed results

The acceptance target is one LayerFit product and one campaign brief producing 15 distinct concepts in three formats: 45 image files. These are requirements from the brief, not verified production metrics.

  • A working web application, source code, setup instructions, and a demonstration of the generation process.
  • A fresh-product test: accept a new product and brief without manually rebuilding the designs.
  • A report covering generation time, AI API costs, remaining manual work, key limitations, and a path to 100+ creatives.
  • Image-quality checks for consistent branding, readable Dutch text, text overflow, and Meta interface safe areas.

Optional extensions in the brief include Shopify product import, background generation, a reusable creative library, background removal, and structured metadata for later ad-performance tracking. These are not presented as implemented features.

No live application, finished creative samples, or campaign results are published here yet. Client financial terms, internal materials, and access details are intentionally omitted.

Where marketing meets automation

This project connects audience research and ad messaging with a repeatable production workflow: clear inputs, constrained AI use, reusable rendering, human review, and measurable operating costs. The goal is to make creative exploration easier without confusing output volume with campaign effectiveness.
