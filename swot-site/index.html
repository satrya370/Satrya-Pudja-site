How it works

You describe the situation

What you're analyzing, the decision it needs to inform, your target market, current condition, internal resources, and known limitations — the same inputs a real strategist would ask for.

A safety guard checks the input

Empty, abusive, or unsupported submissions are rejected before any analysis runs.

Optional research, then synthesis

If you ask for it, the pipeline researches competitors and market context first. Either way, the AI analyst builds the SWOT from your input plus whatever evidence it gathered — never inventing a claim it can't trace back.

A validated report comes back

Four-quadrant SWOT, TOWS strategies (SO, WO, ST, WT), priority actions, and a stated confidence/limitations section — delivered as a document, emailed if you gave an address.

What's actually in the report

SWOT matrix

Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats — each one tied to a stated basis, not a floating adjective.

TOWS strategies

SO, WO, ST, and WT strategies that combine two quadrants into an actual move, plus ranked priority actions.

Assumptions & limitations

What was assumed, what evidence was missing, and what came from your input versus external research — stated explicitly, not hidden.

What the pipeline protects

Decision alignment

The objective comes first, so the analysis answers a concrete decision instead of producing four disconnected lists.

Evidence traceability

Each factor carries its basis and source context, making it clear what came from you, research, or an explicit assumption.

Fail-closed validation

Input and AI responses are checked before the next stage runs, so malformed or unsafe content does not silently become a report.

Actionable delivery

The final document includes TOWS combinations and ranked priority actions, not just observations to interpret later.

What it doesn't do yet

  • It does not replace founder, team, or domain judgment; the report is a structured decision aid.
  • External research is optional. User Information Only mode keeps the analysis grounded strictly in the submitted context.
  • It does not claim certainty where the input or research evidence is incomplete; limitations are stated in the report.
  • The live demo currently runs through a Microsoft Dev Tunnel and may be unavailable when the local n8n host is offline or the tunnel expires.
