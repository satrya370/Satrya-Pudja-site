How it works

You describe the situation

What you're analyzing, the decision it needs to inform, your target market, current condition, internal resources, and known limitations — the same inputs a real strategist would ask for.

A safety guard checks the input

Empty, abusive, or unsupported submissions are rejected before any analysis runs.

Optional research, then synthesis

If you ask for it, the pipeline researches competitors and market context first. Either way, the AI analyst builds the SWOT from your input plus whatever evidence it gathered — never inventing a claim it can't trace back.

A validated report comes back

Four-quadrant SWOT, TOWS strategies (SO, WO, ST, WT), priority actions, and a stated confidence/limitations section — delivered as a document, emailed if you gave an address.

What's actually in the report

SWOT matrix

Strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, and threats — each one tied to a stated basis, not a floating adjective.

TOWS strategies

SO, WO, ST, and WT strategies that combine two quadrants into an actual move, plus ranked priority actions.

Assumptions & limitations

What was assumed, what evidence was missing, and what came from your input versus external research — stated explicitly, not hidden.
