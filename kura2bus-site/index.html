SEO & Analytics · Bachelor thesis · 2026

97 booking clicks. One transaction.

A GA4 study of Kura2Bus's Highlight of Central Bali tour page.

Problem

Visitors clicked Book now, but few completed a purchase. Traffic alone was not explaining the conversion gap.

What I did

Combined stakeholder interviews with GA4 traffic and event analysis. Recommended booking-flow testing, better keyword targeting, and organic content improvements.

Tools

Google Analytics 4 Explore · Event and traffic reports · Stakeholder interviews

Result/Insight

Booking clicks
97
Transactions
1
Organic session length
~5.5× paid

The study identified a post-click conversion gap and channel-quality differences. These are diagnostic findings, not a measured improvement after implementation.
