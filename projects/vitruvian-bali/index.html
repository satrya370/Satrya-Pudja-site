Project overview

Vitruvian Bali offers Reformer Pilates classes in Denpasar. My work focused on the Gatsu location landing page and supporting WordPress website components, connecting web development, technical SEO, local search optimization, analytics integration, and AI-assisted production.

The business objective was a clearer path from discovering the studio to understanding its classes and making an enquiry. The work established website and marketing foundations; this case study does not claim measured traffic, ranking, or lead growth.

Project objectives

  • Develop a responsive, user-friendly Gatsu landing page with clear class information and enquiry pathways.
  • Improve discoverability through technical, on-page, and location-specific SEO.
  • Establish analytics and tracking foundations for future optimization and campaigns.
  • Use AI-assisted HTML/CSS to accelerate iteration without skipping code review or testing.

Website development & implementation

WordPress, Elementor & custom code

  • Developed and customized location-specific sections using Elementor and custom HTML/CSS within WordPress.
  • Worked on desktop and mobile layouts, navigation, content structure, homepage improvements, and blog functionality.
  • Supported WordPress configuration, plugin management, maintenance, and technical troubleshooting.

A business-oriented customer journey

  • Organized location details, class information, and booking-related content around common visitor questions.
  • Worked on call-to-action placement and WhatsApp enquiry pathways to connect interest with contact options.
  • Reviewed content hierarchy, readability, and responsive behavior while adapting components to business and design requirements.

AI was a development assistant, not a production-readiness guarantee. Generated HTML/CSS provided starting points and alternatives; the work required reviewing, adapting, debugging, testing, and validating outputs before use in Elementor.

Technical, on-page & local SEO

AIOSEO & on-page configuration

Worked on SEO titles, meta descriptions, focus keywords, search appearance, and XML sitemap management through All in One SEO. On-page analysis informed heading structure, image optimization, internal linking, and alignment between page content and search intent.

JSON-LD & Schema.org

Worked on structured business, location, contact, and service information using relevant Schema.org vocabulary. LocalBusiness planning used the most appropriate business subtype where applicable. The public page contains Organization and ExerciseGym markup; validation must check both JSON syntax and consistency with visible business details, without assuming rich-result eligibility.

Robots.txt, sitemaps & indexing

Work covered crawl directives, XML sitemap accessibility, crawlability, and indexing configuration. Google Search Console supported URL inspection, sitemap submission workflows, and investigation of indexing issues. A crawlable page or submitted sitemap does not guarantee indexing; current coverage still needs account-level verification.

Local SEO & off-page research

Researched Denpasar Pilates competitors, search intent, and location keywords such as “Reformer Pilates Denpasar” and “Pilates Studio Denpasar.” Applied location-specific content planning and researched business citations, backlink opportunities, and Google Business Profile improvements. These opportunities are strategies, not claims of acquired links or completed profile changes.

Analytics & marketing tracking

Worked on GA4, GTM, and GSC integration and used traffic, user-behavior, acquisition, and organic-search analysis to identify optimization opportunities. The platforms serve different roles:

Google Tag Manager

Centralizes tags and event configuration. The tracking strategy includes WhatsApp link clicks and potential lead events, which require trigger testing, consent checks, and production validation before being treated as collected conversions.

Google Analytics 4

Supports traffic acquisition, engagement, behavior, and event-based analysis. Events can reveal potential conversion opportunities, but clicking WhatsApp alone does not confirm an enquiry, booking, or sale.

Google Search Console

Supports search impressions, clicks, keyword analysis, URL inspection, indexing monitoring, sitemap submission, and technical diagnostics. Search performance and current indexing require access to the verified property.

Integration work is not proof of successful production collection. No private GA4, GTM, or GSC reports are published here; event firing, data quality, and lead attribution remain unverified in this public case study.
