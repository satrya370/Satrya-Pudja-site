Type a topic — a title, or just a rough idea — and this pipeline safety-checks it, researches it with live web search, plans a 7-slide carousel across one of six visual templates, renders each slide as a real PNG with a headless browser, and writes the caption. How it works

Brief comes in

A form: topic, optional detail and keywords, a visual template (or Auto), a research lens, language, and how many slides to aim for. Only an email is required.

AI safety guard checks the topic first

Before any research runs — and before any tokens get spent — a classifier call decides whether the topic is safe for a public demo. Unsafe or empty topics are rejected here, not after a full research pass.

Live web research

A research agent with a real web-search tool call gathers sources, quotes, and data points for the chosen angle — statistics, a case study, common mistakes, or seven other research lenses — and scores each source for recency and authority.

Brief and slide plan get written

The research gets turned into a slide-by-slide plan: a hook and headline for the intro, one step per "isi" slide, a closing slide — mapped onto one of six visual templates (tips, code, visual, editorial, momentum, or a general template).

Every slide gets rendered as a real image

A headless Chromium instance loads each slide's HTML/CSS directly (no temp files) and screenshots it at 1080×1350 — Instagram's carousel size — one PNG per slide.

Caption, log, and delivery

A caption and hashtag pass writes the post copy, the run gets logged to a Google Sheet, and the finished carousel is emailed to the address on the form. A failed run alerts a Telegram channel instead of failing silently.

Six visual templates

Tips

Numbered step slides with a tag and explanation — the template used in the sample above.

Code

Built for technical topics — code-flavored layout for dev-audience content.

Visual

Image-forward layout for topics that lean on a strong visual rather than dense text.

Editorial

A more magazine-style layout for opinion or analysis-driven topics.

Momentum

Built for trend or news-style topics — what's happening and why it matters now.

Auto

Leave the template unset and the planner picks one based on the topic and research.

What it doesn't do yet

  • No direct Instagram posting — you get the PNGs and caption back by email, not an auto-published post.
  • 5 runs per day per email address on the public demo, to keep AI research and rendering costs bounded.
  • Public-demo topics go through an AI safety check first — off-scope or unsafe topics are declined before research starts.
  • Custom image upload isn't wired into the public form yet — image handling is Auto-generate, a URL you supply, or skipped.

Under the hood

74 nodes, running on the same Docker Compose stack on AWS EC2 as the other demos on this site — n8n and a separate Chromium container behind Caddy. Puppeteer connects to Chromium over a WebSocket rather than launching a local browser, since the two run in separate containers.
